## Purpose

Define how Mav manages client relationships from **Closed-Won through renewal or churn**, so every client gets a consistent experience. Every process supports one objective: **help every client achieve measurable business outcomes while maximizing long-term recurring revenue for Mav.**

## Philosophy

- **Customer Success owns the client relationship** — it coordinates Product, Performance, Engineering, Solutions Engineering, Sales, and Leadership to deliver a consistent experience.
- Measured by the client's **long-term success**, not by completing individual tasks.
- Every interaction should move the client toward: successful Go Live → Activation → Healthy → Expansion → Renewal.
- **What it does _not_ own:** product roadmap, feature development, engineering implementation, platform reliability, integration development, performance-optimization strategy, marketing, or new-logo sales. Customer Success _identifies_ issues, _coordinates_ the right team, and _owns the client communication and accountability_ until resolved.

## Priorities (in order)

1. **Prevent churn** — recover at-risk clients before they cancel.
2. **Drive clients to Activation** — remove blockers to the Activated milestone.
3. **Resolve operational issues** — coordinate internal teams on technical/integration problems.
4. **Maintain healthy relationships** — proactive communication and follow-through.
5. **Grow existing accounts** — expansion that increases recurring revenue.
6. **Renew clients.**
7. **Represent the Voice of the Client** internally.

## Lifecycle: statuses & milestones

A client has exactly **one status** at a time but can earn **many milestones**.

**Statuses:** `Onboarding` → `Healthy` ↔ `At-Risk` → (`Churned`). **Milestones:** `Onboarded` → `Activated` → `Expanded` → `Renewed`. The **happy path**: Closed-Won → Onboarding → Go Live → Healthy → Activated → Expansion(s) → Renewal(s).

<Note>
  **Activation** = cumulative **recurring** leads submitted ≥ the client's monthly contracted lead commitment (not time-bound). **Recurring leads** arrive via an active integration; **manually imported / aged leads do not count** toward Activation. A **Transfer** = a qualified consumer connected live to the client's team. These tie directly to the data-model lexicon.
</Note>

## Meeting cadence

- **Weekly:** Onboarding, working-toward-Activation, At-Risk, enterprise, or accounts with active issues.
- **Bi-weekly:** Healthy accounts needing ongoing guidance.
- **Monthly:** mature, low-touch Healthy accounts.

**Standard agenda:** Operational review → Activation review (until Activated) → Account-health review → Performance review (communicate, don't optimize) → Voice of the Client → Expansion & renewal → Action items. **Every meeting is documented in the HubSpot Client Success Meeting object.**

## Troubleshooting

1. Understand the issue → 2. Determine severity → 3. Determine ownership (Customer Success stays the client's point of contact regardless of which team owns the fix) → 4. Communicate → 5. Resolution (root cause identified, solution implemented, client informed, HubSpot updated).

## Guardrails (“what we don't do”)

- No silent escalations — the client hears from us first.
- No removing an account from at-risk without a documented root cause in the HubSpot note.
- No carrying recovery accounts indefinitely — after a second failed extension, Customer Success \+ Sales jointly decide: renegotiate, pause, or churn.
