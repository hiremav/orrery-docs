## What it will do (once the process is documented)

The manual already states that before an account is marked **Churned**: churn reason documented, offboarding checklist completed, internal post-mortem conducted, HubSpot records updated. Once the actual steps are written, this skill will run the checklist, draft the post-mortem from the account's history (mongo \+ Granola), and create the closeout tasks.
