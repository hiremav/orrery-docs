<Info>
  **Skill:** `customer-success:activation-review` · **Owner:** Client Success Manager. The single source of truth the skill hydrates from Orrery. A client is **Activated** when cumulative **recurring** leads ≥ their monthly contracted lead commitment (not time-bound).
</Info>

## Purpose

For a client that hasn't reached **Activated** yet, run the weekly activation review: measure progress, check operational readiness, find what's blocking, and produce next steps. Activation is the leading indicator of long-term retention, so this is priority #2 in Customer Success.

## How Orrery runs this

1. **Resolve the account** and **read `account.next_enabled` first** (decides Classic vs Next collections — see the data-model lexicon).
2. **Confirm the client is pre-Activation.** If cumulative recurring leads already ≥ commitment, say so and stop (they're Activated).
3. Compute and report the items below. **Confirm with the human before creating Todoist tasks.**

## What to compute

- **Activation %** = cumulative **recurring** leads ÷ monthly contracted commitment. Count integration-delivered (recurring) leads only — **manual imports / aged leads don't count.**
- **Lead flow** — is recurring flow active? Is volume increasing (last 7d vs prior 7d)?
- **Operational readiness** — are transfers occurring? first transfer reached? first quote? first policy?
- **Funnel drop-off** (Mixpanel) — volume → connection → duration; where are leads dropping?
- **Activation blockers** — for each, an owner, a next action, and a target resolution date. Common blockers: no recurring lead flow, low volume, integration issues (lead vendor or CRM), transfer delivery issues, client staffing/operational issues, competing texting/automation systems.

## Output

Activation % \+ an operational-readiness checklist (✅/❌) \+ a blockers table (blocker · owner · next action · target date) \+ recommended next steps \+ whether Activation stays a standing agenda item.

## Data sources (engine-aware)

- **Recurring leads** — `leads` (Classic) / `next_contacts` (Next) by `created_at`, recurring only.
- **Transfers / first transfer** — Classic `party_lines` (`outcome: 'successful'`); Next phone-call sessions in `next_sessions` with a `role: :rep` participant. **Funnel** — Mixpanel.

## Gaps

- **Contracted commitment** lives in HubSpot / the Order Form — ask the human until a HubSpot MCP is wired; don't invent it.
- Distinguishing **recurring vs manual-import** leads relies on the lead's originator/source field — confirm it in the lexicon.
