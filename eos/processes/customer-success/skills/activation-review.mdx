<Info>
  **Skill:** `customer-success:activation-review` · **Owner:** Client Success Manager · **Status:** planned (scaffold). This is the weekly activation review run until a client reaches **Activated** (cumulative recurring leads ≥ monthly contracted commitment).
</Info>

## What it does

For a client that hasn't hit Activated yet:

- Compute **activation %** = cumulative **recurring** leads ÷ monthly contracted lead commitment.
- Check **operational readiness**: is recurring lead flow active and increasing? are transfers occurring? first transfer / first quote / first policy reached?
- Pull **funnel drop-off** (volume → connection → duration) from Mixpanel.
- List **activation blockers**, each with an owner, a next action, and a target resolution date.
- Recommend next steps and flag whether to keep Activation a standing agenda item.

## Data sources (engine-aware)

- **Recurring leads** — `leads` (Classic) / `next_contacts` (Next) by `created_at`; count **recurring** (integration-delivered) only — manually imported / aged leads don't count toward Activation.
- **Transfers / first transfer** — Classic `party_lines` (`outcome: 'successful'`); Next phone-call sessions in `next_sessions` with a `role: :rep` participant.
- **Funnel** — Mixpanel. 
- **Context** — recent Granola client-meeting transcripts.

## Gaps

- **Contracted lead commitment** lives in HubSpot / the Order Form — ask the human until a HubSpot MCP is wired.
- Distinguishing **recurring vs manual-import** leads relies on the lead's originator/source — confirm the field in the lexicon.
