  **Skill:** `customer-success:troubleshoot` · **Owner:** Client Success Manager · **Status:** planned (scaffold). Runs the troubleshooting process so issues get the right owner fast — Customer Success stays the client's point of contact regardless of who fixes it.
</Info>

## What it does

1. **Understand** — gather facts (what, when, impact, reproducible?, one client or many?).
2. **Severity** — does it block lead delivery / transfers / the client's business?
3. **Ownership** — route per the table below.
4. **Communicate** — draft a client update (status, next steps, timeline, any client action).
5. **Resolve** — root cause identified, solution implemented, client informed, record updated.

### Ownership routing

| Issue | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Client communication, account health, activation, expansion, renewals | Customer Success |
| CRM & lead-vendor integrations, technical troubleshooting | Solutions Engineering |
| Product bugs, feature requests | Product |
| Platform reliability | Engineering |
| Performance optimization | Performance |

## What it does in Orrery

Classify the issue, assign the owning team, **draft a Linear ticket** (RICE-scored if Mav-owned), and draft the client update. Inspect mongo to help reproduce / scope.

## Gaps

- Resolution logging lives in the **HubSpot Client Success Meeting object** — write-back needs a HubSpot MCP.
