</Info>

## What it does

Produce a one-screen brief for an account following the **standard meeting agenda**:

1. **Operational review** — lead flow, transfers, open support issues, outstanding action items.
2. **Activation review** (if not yet Activated) — activation % and blockers.
3. **Account-health review** — Healthy vs At-Risk signals.
4. **Performance review** — contact rate, transfer rate, quote rate, policy rate (to _communicate_, not optimize).
5. **Voice of the Client** — recent sentiment / feedback from Granola call transcripts.
6. **Expansion & renewal** — any signals or upcoming dates.
7. **Action items** — open Linear tickets / Todoist tasks for the account.

## Data sources

- **mongo** — lead flow, transfers, account config. **Mixpanel** — contact/transfer/quote/policy rates \+ funnel. **Granola** — recent client-meeting sentiment. **Linear / Todoist** — open items.

## Gaps

- The **Client Success Meeting object** (the system of record) lives in **HubSpot** — reading prior notes and writing the post-meeting record needs a HubSpot MCP. Until then this skill _assembles_ the brief; the CSM logs it in HubSpot.
