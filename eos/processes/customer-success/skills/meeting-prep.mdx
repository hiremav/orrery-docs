## Purpose

Walk a CSM into every weekly meeting prepared. Given an account, produce a **one-screen brief** that follows the standard Client Success meeting agenda, sourced from real data. This is the core capacity lever behind Engineer Success — it removes the manual prep from ~50 meetings/week.

## How Orrery runs this

1. **Resolve the account** in mongo (id or name) and **read `account.next_enabled` first** — it decides which collections everything else queries (see the data-model lexicon: `leads`/`party_lines` for Classic, `next_contacts`/`next_sessions` for Next).
2. Assemble the brief, one section per agenda item (below). Source every number; where a number isn't reachable, say so and list it under "Needs the human."
3. Present the brief, then list **open questions for the human** (contracted commitment, renewal date, sentiment confirmation) — don't invent these.

## The brief (standard agenda)

1. **Operational review** — recurring lead flow (last 7d / 30d), transfers (count \+ most recent), open support issues (Linear tickets for the account), outstanding action items (Todoist). Call out any operational blocker.
2. **Activation review** (only if not yet Activated) — activation % = cumulative recurring leads ÷ monthly contracted commitment; top blockers. (Hand off to `customer-success:activation-review` for the deep version.)
3. **Account-health review** — Healthy vs At-Risk read: utilization vs commitment, recurring flow over the trailing 30d, transfers, engagement signals. If at-risk indicators are present, flag them and point to `customer-success:at-risk`.
4. **Performance review** (communicate, don't optimize) — contact rate (conversations ÷ leads), transfer rate (transfers ÷ leads), quote rate, policy rate. Trends vs prior period. Note whether Performance should be engaged.
5. **Voice of the Client** — pull the most recent client meeting from Granola; summarize sentiment \+ any feedback, feature requests, testimonials.
6. **Expansion & renewal** — expansion signals (utilization consistently \> commitment, strong advocacy); upcoming renewal date (HubSpot-gated — ask).
7. **Action items** — consolidated open Linear tickets \+ Todoist tasks for the account.

## Data sources

- **mongo** — lead flow, transfers, account config. **Mixpanel** — contact/transfer/quote/policy rates \+ funnel. **Granola** — recent client-meeting sentiment. **Linear / Todoist** — open items.

## Guardrails & gaps

- **Communicate performance, don't optimize it** — optimization is the Performance team's job.
- Don't invent metrics you can't source — list them under "Needs the human."
- The **Client Success Meeting object** (system of record) is in **HubSpot** — reading prior notes and writing the post-meeting record needs a HubSpot MCP. For now Orrery _assembles_ the brief; the CSM logs it in HubSpot.
