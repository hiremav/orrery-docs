In EOS, an **issue** is anything in the way of the vision: a problem, an obstacle, an opportunity, or an unresolved question. Anyone can raise one at any time. Issues are not to-dos (those are 7-day action items) and not rocks (those are quarterly priorities) — they're the backlog of things that need a decision.

We don't track specific issues on this page — they live in **Ninety** (per team) and are worked in each team's weekly L10. This page explains **how** we solve them and **how every issue is framed**.

## How we solve issues: IDS

Issues are worked in the weekly L10 using **IDS**:

- **Identify** — name the real root issue, not the symptom.
- **Discuss** — everyone says their piece once (no re-litigating, no side conversations).
- **Solve** — make a decision. It becomes a to-do (7-day action) or a rock (quarterly priority).

Issues get solved in L10s, not hallway chats, so the whole team sees the decision. Issues too big for the quarter are parked for the next quarterly planning session.

## Frame every issue by type

Every issue is **prefixed with its type** so the team immediately knows what's being asked of them before discussion starts:

| Prefix | Use it when… |
| --- | --- |
| **Problem** | Something is broken or blocking and needs a fix or a decision. |
| **Brainstorm** | You want the group to generate ideas or options together. |
| **Alignment** | You need everyone on the same page / agreement on a direction. |
| **Approval** | A decision or sign-off is required from the group or an owner. |
| **FYI / Update** | Informational — no decision needed; just shared awareness. |
| **Opportunity** | A possibility worth pursuing that the team should weigh. |

Example: _“Approval — move the Growth L10 to Mondays”_ or _“Problem — at-risk metric is firing false positives.”_

## Where issues live (and how Orrery sees them)

- **Ninety** is the system of record (per team). Each team works its list in its weekly L10.
- **Orrery** references current issues via the weekly **L10 transcripts in Granola** (Leadership Wednesdays, Growth Tuesdays, Brand Mondays) — the IDS segment is the back half of each meeting.
- **Coming soon:** a two-way sync between **Ninety issues** and **Todoist** projects via Ninety's [native Todoist integration](https://help.ninety.io/en/articles/15505684-todoist-native-integration-beta). Once it's on, issues and to-dos stay in lockstep — and Orrery can read and act on them through the Todoist MCP.
