In EOS, an **issue** is anything in the way of the vision: a problem, an obstacle, an opportunity, or an unresolved question. Anyone can raise one at any time. Issues are not to-dos (those are 7-day action items) and not rocks (those are quarterly priorities) — they're the backlog of things that need a decision.

Issues are worked in the weekly L10 using **IDS**:

- **Identify** — name the real root issue, not the symptom.
- **Discuss** — everyone says their piece once.
- **Solve** — make a decision, which usually becomes a to-do or a rock.

Issues get solved in L10s, not hallway chats, so the whole team sees the decision. Long-term issues (too big for this quarter) are parked for the next quarterly planning session.
