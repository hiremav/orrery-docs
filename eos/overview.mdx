Mav runs on **EOS** (the Entrepreneurial Operating System) — our human-compatible operating system for running the company. EOS isn't one flat thing: it's a **hierarchy of instances**, each with its own weekly L10 meeting.

## The hierarchy

- **Leadership** runs the **company-level** instance — the full Vision/Traction Organizer (Vision, People, Process) plus the leadership team's own Scorecard, Issues, and Rocks.
- **Teams beneath leadership** each run **their own** L10 with **their own** Scorecard, Issues, and Rocks. **Growth** is live today; **Brand** and **Product** are coming. Their rocks ladder up to the company rocks; they share the company Vision.

## What's shared vs. per-team

| EOS component | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| **Vision** (V/TO), **People** (accountability), **Process** (the Mav Way) | Company-level (Leadership) — shared by all teams |
| **Scorecard**, **Issues**, **Rocks** | Per team — Leadership has its own; each team has its own |
| **L10 cadence** | Per team — each team runs its own weekly meeting |

## L10 cadence & where the freshest readout lives

The live scorecard/issues/rocks are in **Ninety**; the freshest _discussion_ is each team's weekly L10 transcript in **Granola**.

| Instance | L10 | Freshest source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leadership | Wednesdays 10am PT | Leadership L10 transcript (Granola) |
| Growth | Tuesdays 10am PT | Growth Team L10 transcript (Granola) |
| Brand | Mondays 1pm PT | Brand Team L10 transcript (Granola) |
| Product | (cadence TBC) | Product Team L10 transcript (Granola) |

<Note>
  When answering a question about company or team state, first decide **which instance** it's about (company/leadership vs. a specific team), then read that instance's Scorecard / Issues / Rocks — and pull the freshest numbers from that team's L10 transcript in Granola.
</Note>
