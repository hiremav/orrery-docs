## Shared vs. per-team

- **Vision** and **People** are **company-wide** — one shared instance.
- **Scorecard** and **Rocks** are run **per team**. Leadership runs the company-level instance; teams beneath it (Growth today; Brand and Product coming) run their own. Team rocks ladder up to the company rocks; every team shares the company Vision.
- **Issues** is a single page on the IDS method and how we frame issues; specific issues live in Ninety and each team's L10.
- **Processes** is the Mav Way: a company-level overview plus each department's core processes.

## Instances & L10 cadence

The live Scorecard/Issues/Rocks are in **Ninety**; the freshest _discussion_ is each team's weekly L10 transcript in **Granola**.
