The weekly numbers that tell us, at a glance, whether the business is healthy.

<Info>
  **Source of truth: [Ninety](https://ninety.io).** Weekly goals and actuals are entered there by each owner and reviewed every L10 (Level 10 meeting). There is currently **no programmatic access to Ninety** from Orrery, so this page defines _what_ each measurable is — not its live value. **Last exported from Ninety: week of Jun 24–30, 2026.**
</Info>

## Toward real-time measurables

The goal is for Orrery to read each measurable from its **live source** rather than a weekly manual entry — so the scorecard is always current and we can drill into any number on demand:

- **Revenue & retention** (ARR pace, committed/closed ARR, churn) → **ChartMogul**
- **Sales funnel** (close rate, meetings, deals moved, pipeline coverage, MQLs) → **HubSpot**
- **Infrastructure** (lead-engagement latency, uptime) → **New Relic**
- **Deliverability** → **Bandwidth**
- **Performance deltas** (positive-intent, win-rate) → the **Mav production database** / Mixpanel
- **Initiatives** (green/yellow/red) → **Ninety** (the weekly pulse)

Until those connections exist, the interim source is the **weekly leadership L10 transcript in Granola** — the scorecard is read aloud near the top of every L10, so Orrery can pull the latest actuals from there.
