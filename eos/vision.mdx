## Core values

The non-negotiables. We hire and fire by these.

1. **Figure it Out** — Own the problem, go hunting for the solution. We don't wait for someone to tell us what to do.
2. **Hard Things First** — Attack the hardest work first. Don't nibble. The hardest problem on your plate should be the first thing you attack.
3. **Strong Opinions, Held Loosely** — Debate hard, pivot when new info comes in. Strong minds, low ego.
4. **Radical Honesty** — Say what needs to be said early, with respect. It's what makes us hard to kill — companies die from avoiding the truth for too long, not from one big mistake.
